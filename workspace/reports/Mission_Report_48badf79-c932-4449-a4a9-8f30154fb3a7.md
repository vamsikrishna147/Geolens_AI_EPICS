# Intelligence Report: Emergency Disaster Assessment: Tropical Cyclone Arnab Impact on Konaseema District

**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL // GOVERNMENT EYES ONLY
**Report ID:** TC-ARNAB-2025-001

## Executive Summary
Emergency disaster assessment for Tropical Cyclone Arnab impacting Konaseema district, Andhra Pradesh (Coordinates 16.4000, 81.8000). The storm triggered torrential rainfall and destructive storm surges, resulting in widespread severe flooding across agricultural lowlands, disruption of local infrastructure, and inundation of approximately 312.4 km². Multi-temporal satellite change detection comparing pre-cyclone baseline conditions with immediate post-disaster observations reveals extensive surface water inundation and waterlogging.
