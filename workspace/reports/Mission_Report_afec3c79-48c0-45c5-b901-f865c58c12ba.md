# Intelligence Report: Emergency Disaster Assessment: Tropical Cyclone Arnab

**Classification:** UNCLASSIFIED // OFFICIAL USE ONLY
**Report ID:** TC-ARNAB-2024-001

## Executive Summary
Extensive inundation and surface water expansion across Andhra Pradesh, India, following Tropical Cyclone Arnab. Water body extent surged from a baseline of 21,046.33 km² to 53,579.92 km² during the event window (November 2024), yielding an incremental inundation area of 32,533.59 km². Tropical Cyclone Arnab triggered torrential rainfall and severe coastal storm surges, resulting in catastrophic flooding across low-lying districts of Andhra Pradesh, submerging over 32,500 square kilometers of additional land beyond normal baseline water bodies.
