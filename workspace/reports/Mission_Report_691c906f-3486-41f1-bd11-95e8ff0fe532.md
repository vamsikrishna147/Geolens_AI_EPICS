# Intelligence Report: Earth Observation & Intelligence Brief: Landscape Dynamics and Urban Expansion in Ratnagiri (2018–2024)

**Classification:** UNCLASSIFIED / FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY
**Report ID:** REP-RATNAGIRI-2024-001

## Executive Summary
This executive intelligence report synthesizes multi-temporal satellite Earth Observation analysis across Ratnagiri, Maharashtra from 2018 to 2024. Multi-spectral imagery reveals stable overall forest and rural vegetation canopy (NDVI shifting marginally from 0.3711 to 0.3567, representing a minor -3.88% delta). However, urban land cover analysis shows a rapid surge in built-up area, expanding from 285.56 km² to 413.93 km²—a growth of +128.37 km² (+44.95%). This footprint expansion is concentrated along coastal transport corridors and peri-urban zones due to port development, infrastructure projects, and residential growth. Tiered Action Plan: 1. Immediate Emergency Measures: Establish active monitoring buffers around high-risk peri-urban construction zones to prevent unchecked encroachment into coastal eco-sensitive zones. 2. Medium-Term Recovery & Zoning: Implement structured regional land-use zoning controls to balance economic infrastructure expansion with agricultural and mangrove protection. 3. Long-Term Policy: Mandate satellite-based quarterly LULC compliance tracking into Maharashtra's municipal urban planning frameworks.
